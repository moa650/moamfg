Welcome to MOA.

This is an R&D project tracking the frontier of bipedal robotics — inspired by the Moa, an extinct flightless bird of New Zealand, and by the research coming out of labs like Agility Robotics, Boston Dynamics, Figure, and NVIDIA.

Look around. More coming soon.
